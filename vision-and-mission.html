OUR SPIRITUAL FOUNDATION

Vision & Mission

Saving lost souls in Christ, planting vibrant churches, and nurturing communities through faith, prayer, and education.

The Divine Calling & Founding

Thirunivasam Gospel Ministries (formerly known as Holy Heart Prayer Squad) was established by Rev. Dr. Edward Clement in 1983 following a divine vision received to save people lost in Christ.

Registered under the Government of India as a Religious Charitable Trust, our organization supports dedicated missionaries who sacrifice their lives to plant new churches in expanding regions.

Spiritual & Educational Initiatives

Our ministries encompass various programs including Thirunivasam Bible College, Sunday Bible schools, free tailoring vocational training, personal life counseling, and aid for poor families.

Chief Pastor

Rev. Dr. Edward Clement

Founder & Chief Pastor, Thirunivasam Church

After completing his post-graduate studies in literature, Rev. Dr. Edward Clement turned his life to Christ through a personal revelation. He holds multiple theological degrees including M.Div., M.Th. from Ebenezer Theological Seminary (under Dr. C. Thomas) and D.D from Apollos Bible University. He leads Thirunivasam Gospel Ministries alongside his wife Mrs. Sreeja and their three children.
